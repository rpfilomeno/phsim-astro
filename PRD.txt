# Project Requirements Document (PRD) & Creative Brief
## Midgarts PH: The Pearl Archipelago (Republic Server Patch Portal)

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**Midgarts PH** is a satirical, high-fidelity web portal that gamifies real-world Philippine socio-political events, infrastructure dilemmas, macro-economic realities, and pop-culture phenomena through the lens of early-2000s **Ragnarok Online (Gravity / Level Up! Games)** patch notes, server status bulletins, and community forums.

By re-framing the country as a massive, chaotic, high-ping MMORPG sandbox ("Republic Server Ver 7107.25"), *Midgarts PH* delivers sharp social commentary wrapped in pixel-nostalgic UI, authentic game mechanics, and relatable Filipino cultural tropes.

---

### 2. Core Themes & Creative Metaphors

| Real-World Phenomenon | In-Game MMORPG Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **7,107 Islands & Regional Diversity** | Sharded Realms (`Luzon-01`, `Visayas-Archipelago`, `Mindanao-SouthGate`) |
| **EDSA Traffic, Commuter Struggles & MRT Breakdowns** | Severe debuffs to Acolyte *Agility Up*, Merchant cart overload penalties, EDSA Carousel zone lag |
| **PUV / Jeepney Modernization Program** | Forced class upgrade to *Euro-4 Modern Mado Carts*, franchise permits, anti-Colorum GM patrols |
| **Agri-Inflation & Food Smuggling (Onions, Rice)** | Exploitative *Golden Sibuyas* market hoarding, Subic Port cold-storage dungeon raids, price caps on *Sinangag* |
| **Philippine Heat Index & El Niño** | 44°C environmental heat debuff; introduction of *Halo-Halo* consumable buff (+50 Heat Resist) |
| **Utility Outages (Meralco, Maynilad)** | Rolling blackouts degrading Alchemist potion brewing; mandatory collection of blue water drums (*Timba at Tabo*) |
| **P-Pop Wave & BINI Craze** | Synchronized party emote `/pantropiko` (+5 ASPD, +10% party morale), limited-edition *Cherry On Top* headgear |
| **LTO Fixers & Red Tape** | *Judicial GM Ban Logs* sanctioning unauthorized guild fixers, counterfeit Cart Driver Licenses, RFID scalpers |

### 3. Target Audience & Personas

1. **The Millennial Ex-RO Grinder (Ages 28–42)**
   - Nostalgic gamers who spent their youth in internet cafes (*lanshop* era, 2002–2010), familiar with RO sound effects, Kafra ladies, and Zeny grinds.
2. **The Modern Commuter & Social Observer (Ages 20–35)**
   - Daily MRT/bus commuters and netizens who engage with Philippine satirical pages (e.g., *The Professional Heckler*, satire meme communities).
3. **Game Designers & UI/UX Engineers**
   - Appreciators of retro-skeuomorphic UI design, 2000s desktop OS window chrome, and contextual lore-building.

### 5. Detailed Feature Specifications

#### 5.1. Patch Note Structure
- **Format**: `[Tag] Topic: Real World Event Translated Into RO Mechanics`
- **Changelog Attributes**:
  - *Affected Classes*: (e.g., Merchant, Acolyte, Novice, Bard/Dancer)
  - *Stat Multipliers*: Numeric buffs/nerfs (e.g., `-15% HP`, `+20 LUK`, `Cooldown +5s`)
  - *GM Dev Notes*: Satirical commentary explaining the design rationale.

#### 5.2. Visual & Audio Design Direction
- **Typography**: EB Garamond paired with monospace system fonts (Courier/Consolas) and classic pixel-style serif/sans accents.
- **Palette**:
  - *Parchment Surface*: `#FFF9ED` / `#FAF3E3` (RO quest window parchment)
  - *Navy Chrome*: `#2B5B84` / `#1A3A54` (RO client title bar & border)
  - *Alert Red*: Crimson `#8B1E2D` (Maintenance & urgent banner warnings)
  - *Gold Accents*: `#D4A346` (Zeny counters, special drops, MVP notifications)
- **UI Skeuomorphism**: Beveled buttons, inset panels, classic retro window minimize/maximize/close icons, pixelated badge chips.

#### 5.3. Interactivity & Micro-Interactions
- Expandable patch sections with toggleable GM commentary.
- Live soundboard triggers (e.g., iconic RO leveling chime, failed refine sound, Kafra greeting).
- Filterable patch categories: `All`, `Transport & Commute`, `Agri-Economy`, `Pop Culture & Events`, `Judicial / Ban Logs`.
